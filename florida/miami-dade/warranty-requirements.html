Common Questions

What warranties are required for Miami-Dade HVHZ windows and doors? +

Typical warranties include: Manufacturer product warranty (10-20 years for glass, lifetime for frames), installation warranty from the contractor, and optional extended warranties. Check project specifications for specific requirements.

Does the warranty cover hurricane damage? +

Manufacturer warranties typically cover defects in materials and workmanship, not damage from storms. However, if products fail within their rated design pressure, that may be a warranty claim. Review the specific warranty terms carefully.

How do I register warranties for HVHZ products? +

Most manufacturers require warranty registration within 30-90 days of installation. Collect all product serial numbers, installation dates, and project information. Submit registration online or via mail to the manufacturer. Keep copies of all registration confirmations.

What voids a warranty on HVHZ products? +

Common warranty voids include: improper installation not per NOA requirements, modifications to the product, exposure to chemicals not intended for the product, failure to maintain per manufacturer guidelines, and installation by non-certified installers (if required).
