Common Questions

Can I substitute products in a Miami-Dade HVHZ project? +

Yes, but the substitute product must meet or exceed the original specifications. This includes design pressure ratings, impact resistance, and must have a valid NOA number. You may need updated wind load calculations if specs change significantly.

What specs must match when substituting products? +

Key specs include: Design Pressure (positive and negative), Impact Rating (Large Missile Level), Wind Zone (HVHZ), Water Penetration resistance, Air Infiltration rating, and valid NOA number with current expiration date.

Do I need new calculations when substituting products? +

If the substitute product has different design pressure ratings or you are changing sizes significantly, you may need updated wind load calculations to verify the new product meets requirements. This is especially true if the new product has lower ratings.

Who needs to approve a product substitution? +

The architect or engineer of record must approve substitutions. For significant changes, the building department may also need to review. Submit a formal substitution request with both product specifications for comparison.
