Bid Questions Answered

How much do wind load calculations cost for contractor bids? +
Wind load calculation costs vary. Traditional engineering firms charge $300-800 per calculation. With WindLoad.co, contractors get unlimited calculations for a monthly subscription, making it affordable to calculate every bid accurately.
Should I include wind load costs as an allowance? +
Always include wind load costs as a line item in your bid, not as an allowance. This shows clients you understand Miami-Dade requirements and protects you from cost overruns. HVHZ-rated products cost 15-40% more than standard products.
What wind load items belong in my Miami-Dade bid? +
Miami-Dade contractor bids should include: wind load engineering calculations, PE seal fees, HVHZ-rated product premiums, hurricane-rated hardware, impact-resistant glazing, and enhanced fastening requirements. Each affects total project cost.
How do HVHZ requirements affect project bids? +
HVHZ requirements typically add 15-40% to window and door costs, plus additional labor for specialized installation. Contractors who do not account for these requirements often face significant budget overruns mid-project.
