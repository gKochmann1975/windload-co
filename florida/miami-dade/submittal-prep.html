Common Questions

What documents do I need for a Miami-Dade HVHZ submittal? +

A complete HVHZ submittal typically includes: Product cut sheets, NOA documents for each product, wind load calculations, installation details, shop drawings if required, PE sealed documents when required, and a submittal cover sheet listing all items.

How should I organize my submittal package? +

Organize by section: Cover sheet with index, then each product in order (windows, doors, roofing, etc.). For each product include: cut sheet, NOA, installation details. Put wind load calculations and PE sealed documents in a separate section.

What are common reasons for submittal rejection? +

Common rejections include: Expired NOA documents, missing installation details, design pressures not meeting calculated requirements, incomplete product specifications, and missing PE seals when required by the building department.

When do I need a PE seal on my submittal? +

PE seals are typically required for custom engineering, when product ratings are close to calculated requirements, for large commercial projects, and when specified by the architect or required by the building department.
