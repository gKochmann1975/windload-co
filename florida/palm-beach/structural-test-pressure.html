Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about structural testing and safety factors.

Why are windows tested at 1.5x their design pressure?
+
The 1.5x safety factor ensures products can handle loads beyond their rating without failure. A DP-50 window tested at 75 PSF proves it has margin to handle real-world conditions including gusts, debris impacts, and aging without catastrophic failure.
What is ASTM E330 structural testing?
+
ASTM E330 is the standard test method for structural performance of exterior windows, doors, and curtain walls. Products are placed in a chamber and subjected to uniform static air pressure differences, held for 10 seconds at test pressure in both directions.
What happens if a product fails structural testing?
+
If a product shows permanent deformation, glass breakage, hardware failure, or any breach during testing, it fails. The manufacturer must redesign and retest. Products can't be sold with a DP rating they haven't proven they can exceed by 1.5x.
How long is structural test pressure applied?
+
Per ASTM E330, the test pressure is held for a minimum of 10 seconds in each direction (positive and negative). The product must maintain integrity throughout with no permanent deflection exceeding allowable limits.
