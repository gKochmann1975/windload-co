Common Questions

Exterior Door FAQs

What wind ratings do exterior doors need in Palm Beach County?

Exterior doors in Palm Beach County need design pressure ratings of +/-40 to +/-65 PSF for residential entry doors, and higher ratings for commercial applications. All doors must meet FBC requirements and have valid Florida Product Approval or NOA certification for the required pressure rating.

Do garage doors need special wind ratings in Palm Beach?

Yes, garage doors are particularly vulnerable to wind damage due to their large size and require design pressures of +/-35 to +/-55 PSF depending on door dimensions and location. In the WBDR, impact-rated garage doors or approved protection systems are required.

What is the difference between residential and commercial door requirements?

Commercial doors typically require higher wind ratings (+/-60 to +/-90 PSF), heavier-duty hardware and hinges, and may need additional features like panic hardware, automatic closers, and fire ratings. Commercial applications also require more stringent testing documentation.

How much do wind-rated exterior doors cost in Palm Beach County?

Residential entry doors cost $1,500-$5,000 installed. Wind-rated garage doors range from $1,200-$4,500 depending on size. Commercial doors cost $2,500-$8,000 depending on size and rating requirements. Impact-rated versions typically add 30-50% to base prices.
