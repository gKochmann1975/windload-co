Common Questions

Conservatory Glazing FAQs

What glazing requirements apply to conservatories in Palm Beach County?

Conservatories in Palm Beach County must meet FBC glazing requirements including wind load ratings of +/-45 to +/-75 PSF depending on location and exposure. Roof glazing requires additional consideration for uplift pressures. Impact-rated glazing is required throughout in WBDR areas.

Can I build a glass conservatory in Palm Beach HVHZ areas?

Yes, conservatories can be built in Palm Beach County including coastal areas, but require impact-rated glazing throughout, engineered structural framing, and PE-stamped plans. All glass must meet ASTM E1996 large missile impact requirements for the Wind-Borne Debris Region.

What is the difference between conservatory glazing types?

Lean-to conservatories use single-slope roofs with standard glazing systems suited for attached structures. Victorian styles feature curved glass with complex ornate framing. Modern conservatories use structural glazing with minimal visible frames for maximum glass area and architectural impact.

How much do conservatories cost in Palm Beach County?

Lean-to conservatories cost $150-250 per square foot installed. Victorian style conservatories range $250-400/SF due to complex curved glazing. Modern structural glass conservatories cost $350-600/SF. A typical 200 SF conservatory runs $30,000-$120,000 depending on style and specifications.
