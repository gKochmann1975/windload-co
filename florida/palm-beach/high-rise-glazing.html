Common Questions

High-Rise Glazing FAQs

What wind loads apply to high-rise buildings in Palm Beach County?

High-rise buildings in Palm Beach County experience increased wind pressures at elevation. Design pressures can exceed +/-100 PSF on upper floors of tall buildings, compared to +/-50 PSF at ground level. All glazing must meet FBC requirements including impact rating in WBDR areas.

At what height do elevated wind load requirements apply?

Wind pressures increase significantly above 60 feet. Buildings over 4 stories typically require enhanced glazing systems. The most critical wind pressures occur in the upper 20% of the building height and at corners where pressures can be 50% higher than at center wall locations.

What glazing systems are used in Palm Beach high-rises?

High-rise buildings use three main systems: punch windows installed in concrete or masonry wall openings, stick-built curtain walls assembled on-site piece by piece, and unitized curtain walls pre-fabricated in a factory. Unitized systems dominate new construction for quality control and installation speed.

How much does high-rise glazing cost in Palm Beach County?

Punch window systems cost $75-120 per square foot installed. Stick curtain wall systems range $100-175/SF. Unitized curtain wall systems cost $150-275/SF. Total glazing packages for high-rise towers can range from $2-10 million depending on building size and specifications.
