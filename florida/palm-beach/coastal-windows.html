Common Questions

Coastal Window FAQs

What special requirements apply to coastal windows in Palm Beach County?

Coastal windows in Palm Beach County must meet HVHZ wind load requirements (+/-60 to +/-90 PSF), impact rating per ASTM E1996, corrosion resistance for salt spray exposure, and enhanced water infiltration resistance. All glazing within the Wind-Borne Debris Region requires impact certification.

How close to the ocean do I need marine-grade windows?

Marine-grade finishes are recommended within 1,500 feet of the ocean and required by many manufacturers for warranty coverage within 1,000 feet. Salt spray can travel several miles inland during storms, so premium finishes benefit properties up to 3 miles from the coast.

What is the difference between coastal window grades?

Standard coastal windows use basic impact glass and standard finishes suited for inland areas. Marine-grade windows feature enhanced corrosion protection with AAMA 2605 finishes and improved seals. Premium oceanfront systems add heavy-duty frames, triple-laminated glass, and lifetime corrosion warranties.

How much do coastal windows cost in Palm Beach County?

Standard coastal impact windows cost $55-80 per square foot installed. Marine-grade systems range from $75-110/SF. Premium oceanfront systems cost $100-160/SF. A typical beachfront home window package runs $40,000-$100,000 depending on size and specifications required.
