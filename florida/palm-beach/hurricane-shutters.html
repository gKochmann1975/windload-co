Common Questions

Hurricane Shutter FAQs

Do hurricane shutters reduce insurance costs in Palm Beach?

Yes. Florida law requires insurers to offer discounts for hurricane mitigation. Code-compliant shutters in Palm Beach typically qualify for 8-15% premium reductions, potentially saving $800-2,000 annually depending on your policy value and coverage.

What wind speed rating do shutters need in Palm Beach County?

Most of Palm Beach County requires shutters rated for 150-170 mph wind speeds depending on your specific location. Coastal areas may require higher ratings up to 180 mph. All shutters must pass large missile impact testing per Florida Building Code.

Which hurricane shutter type is best for Palm Beach homes?

Accordion and roll-down shutters are most popular for Palm Beach homes due to ease of deployment and aesthetic appeal. Panel shutters offer the lowest upfront cost, while colonial shutters provide permanent architectural enhancement that's always visible.

Do hurricane shutters require permits in Palm Beach County?

Yes. All hurricane shutter installations in Palm Beach County require building permits. You'll need product approval documentation (Florida Product Approval number), and installations must follow the manufacturer's approved specifications exactly.
