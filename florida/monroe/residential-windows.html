Code Requirements

Monroe County Window Standards

What your windows must meet for Florida Keys compliance

💨

Wind Design

FBC 2024 Section 1620

All windows must meet design wind speeds for the Florida Keys HVHZ region.

175-185 mph design wind speed required based on specific location and risk category.

Exposure Category D applies to most Keys locations due to flat terrain and water exposure.

🎯

Impact Testing

TAS 201, 202, 203

Windows in windborne debris regions must pass large missile impact testing.

9-lb 2x4 @ 50 fps impact test followed by 9,000 pressure cycles at design load.

Glass must remain in frame without penetration after impact.

📜

Product Approval

FL Statute 553.842

All window products must have current Florida approval documentation.

Miami-Dade NOA or Florida Product Approval with HVHZ designation required.

Installation per manufacturer's approved details with proper anchorage.

🏗

Permit Requirements

Monroe County Building

Building permits required for all window replacements involving structural changes.

Submit: Product approvals, contractor license, and wind load calculations.

Some areas require additional FEMA compliance for flood zone properties.
