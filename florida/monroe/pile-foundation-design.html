Common Questions

Pile Foundation FAQs

How deep do piles need to go in Florida Keys coral rock?
+
In Monroe County coral rock, piles typically need 8-12 feet of embedment for residential construction. The actual depth depends on the coral density, building loads, and wind uplift requirements. Harder coral formations may achieve required capacity at shallower depths.
What type of piles work best in Florida Keys coral?
+
Concrete piles (12-14 inch square) and steel pipe piles are most common in the Keys. The coral rock often requires pre-drilling before driving. Treated timber piles are also used for lighter residential loads but require marine-grade treatment for saltwater exposure.
How is pile capacity calculated for Keys construction?
+
Pile capacity in coral rock is calculated using skin friction along the pile shaft plus end bearing on the coral. A geotechnical report determines coral strength. Typical allowable capacities range from 20-60 tons depending on pile size and embedment depth.
Do I need a geotechnical report for pile foundations in Monroe County?
+
Yes, Monroe County requires a geotechnical investigation for pile foundations. The report determines coral rock elevation, strength, and recommended pile type and embedment. This is essential for permit approval and proper foundation design.
