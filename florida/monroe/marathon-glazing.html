Common Questions

Marathon Glazing FAQs

Expert answers to glazing questions specific to Marathon and the Middle Keys.

What wind speed is Marathon FL designed for?
Marathon is in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) with a basic wind speed of 180 mph. All glazing systems must be designed and tested to meet these stringent requirements under the Florida Building Code.
Do commercial storefronts in Marathon need impact glazing?
Yes, all commercial storefronts in Marathon require either impact-rated glazing or approved hurricane protection systems. Impact glazing is preferred for commercial applications due to 24/7 protection and insurance benefits.
What design pressure is required for Marathon storefronts?
Commercial storefronts in Marathon typically require minimum design pressures of +60/-90 PSF for Exposure C locations, increasing to +75/-110 PSF for waterfront Exposure D locations. Specific requirements vary by building height and location.
Are there special glazing requirements for Marathon oceanfront properties?
Yes, oceanfront properties in Marathon face Exposure D conditions requiring higher design pressures, marine-grade finishes (AAMA 2605), stainless steel hardware, and enhanced corrosion protection. Additional flood zone requirements may also apply.
