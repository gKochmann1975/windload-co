Common Questions

Wood Pile FAQs

What treatment is required for wood piles in Florida Keys saltwater?
+
Wood piles in Monroe County saltwater require marine-grade treatment. CCA (Chromated Copper Arsenate) at 2.5 pcf retention is the gold standard for marine piles. ACZA (Ammoniacal Copper Zinc Arsenate) is also approved. Standard ACQ is not sufficient for saltwater exposure.
How long do treated wood piles last in the Florida Keys?
+
Properly treated marine piles can last 25-40 years in Keys waters. The splash zone (between low and high tide) sees the most decay. Untreated wood may fail in just 5-10 years. Regular inspection and wrapping can extend life significantly.
What are the treatment zones on a wood pile?
+
Wood piles have three critical zones: below mud line (protected by soil), submerged zone (underwater, moderate attack), and splash zone (between tides, maximum attack). The splash zone requires the highest treatment levels due to constant wetting and drying with salt exposure.
Can I use pressure-treated lumber piles from the hardware store?
+
No. Hardware store pressure-treated lumber uses ACQ or MCA treatments designed for ground contact, not saltwater immersion. Marine piles need CCA or ACZA treatment at marine retention levels (2.5 pcf). Using residential-grade treatment in saltwater leads to rapid failure.
