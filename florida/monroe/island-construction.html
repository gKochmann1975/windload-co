Common Questions

Island Construction FAQs

What unique challenges affect island construction windows in the Keys?

Island construction in the Keys faces 360-degree marine exposure, limited access for oversized materials, higher transportation costs, and severe salt spray conditions. Windows must be designed for all-around wind exposure and maximum corrosion resistance with premium marine-grade materials.

How does marine exposure differ for island versus mainland Keys construction?

Island properties face marine exposure from multiple directions, unlike mainland properties with one-sided ocean exposure. This requires premium marine-grade finishes (AAMA 2605 minimum), 316 stainless steel hardware, and enhanced sealants rated for 360-degree salt exposure.

Are there size limitations for windows on remote Keys islands?

Yes, remote islands with boat-only access have practical size limits based on vessel capacity and dock capabilities. Windows may need to be sized for barge transport or broken into manageable units. Planning for delivery logistics is essential during the design phase.

Do island properties require different wind load calculations?

Island properties typically require Exposure D calculations from all directions due to open water surroundings. This can result in higher design pressures than mainland properties with some wind shelter. PE calculations should account for worst-case wind approach from any direction.
