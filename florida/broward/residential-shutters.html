Common Questions

Residential Shutter FAQs

What type of hurricane shutter is best for Broward County homes?

The best shutter depends on your budget and priorities. Roll-down shutters offer the best convenience and protection but cost the most ($35-65/SF). Accordion shutters provide excellent value at $20-35/SF. Bahama and colonial shutters add curb appeal. Storm panels are most affordable but require manual installation.

Do hurricane shutters require permits in Broward County?

Yes, all hurricane shutter installations in Broward County require building permits. The shutters must have a valid NOA (Notice of Acceptance) from Miami-Dade Product Control and be installed per manufacturer specifications. Inspections verify proper installation.

What design pressure rating do shutters need in Broward?

Hurricane shutters in Broward County must meet the same design pressure requirements as impact windows, typically +/-45 to +/-75 PSF depending on building height and window location. All shutters must also pass the large missile impact test (9-lb 2x4 at 34 mph).

Do shutters or impact windows give better insurance discounts?

Both options can qualify for similar insurance discounts (typically 8-15%) when properly documented with a wind mitigation inspection. Impact windows may provide slightly higher credits as they offer 24/7 protection. Contact your insurance company for specific credit amounts.
