Common Questions

Bahama Shutter FAQs

What wind rating do Bahama shutters need in Broward County?

Bahama shutters must meet the same design pressure requirements as the openings they protect, typically +/-45 to +/-70 PSF. They must pass large missile impact testing and have a valid NOA from Miami-Dade Product Control.

Can Bahama shutters provide shade when not in storm mode?

Yes! This is one of Bahama shutters' best features. Prop them open at a 30-45 degree angle to block direct sun while allowing light and breezes. They work like permanent awnings that can close for storms.

What size windows work with Bahama shutters?

Bahama shutters work best on windows up to 6 feet wide. Larger openings may require multiple panels or alternative shutter types. They're ideal for standard residential windows but may not suit very large picture windows.

How do Bahama shutters compare to accordion shutters?

Bahama shutters cost more ($25-45/SF vs $20-35/SF) but add daily functionality and aesthetic value. They provide shade year-round and are always ready for storms. Accordions are purely storm protection that fold to the sides when not in use.
