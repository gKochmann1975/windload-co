Common Questions

Roll-Down Shutter FAQs

How much do motorized roll-down shutters cost in Broward County?

Motorized roll-down shutters typically cost $35-65 per square foot installed. A typical home with 200 SF of openings would cost $7,000-13,000 including permits, motor, and professional installation. Premium systems with smart features cost more.

Can roll-down shutters be operated during a power outage?

Yes, most roll-down shutters include manual override options. Tubular motors have crank access points for manual operation. Battery backup systems are also available that can power the motor for multiple cycles during outages.

How long do roll-down shutter motors last?

Quality tubular motors typically last 15-20 years with proper maintenance. External drive units can last 20+ years. Annual lubrication and testing is recommended. Most manufacturers offer 5-10 year motor warranties.

Are roll-down shutters good for home security?

Yes, roll-down shutters provide excellent security protection. The heavy-duty aluminum slats and locking guide tracks make forced entry extremely difficult. Many Broward homeowners deploy them when traveling or as daily security for ground-floor windows.
