Code Requirements

Palm Beach Commercial Glazing Standards

Mandatory specifications per Florida Building Code 2024 and HVHZ requirements

💨

Wind Design Criteria

FBC 2024 Section 1620

Commercial glazing must be designed for ultimate wind speed based on risk category and exposure.

150-175 mph

Design wind speed range for Palm Beach commercial buildings

Risk Category III/IV buildings (hospitals, schools) require enhanced design pressures with importance factor of 1.15.

🎯

Impact Protection

TAS 201, 202, 203

All glazing in HVHZ windborne debris regions requires missile impact testing.

9-lb @ 50 fps

Large missile test: 2x4 lumber impact requirement

Followed by 9,000 cycles of positive and negative pressure at design load. Glass must remain in frame without penetration.

❄

Thermal Performance

Energy Code Section C402

Commercial glazing must meet prescriptive or performance-based energy requirements.

U-0.50 / SHGC 0.40

Maximum values for Climate Zone 1A (prescriptive)

Performance path allows trade-offs. High-performance glazing often exceeds these values significantly.

📜

Product Approval

FL Statute 553.842

All glazing products require current approval documentation.

Miami-Dade NOA or FL PA

Required product approval with HVHZ designation

Installation must follow manufacturer's tested and approved details. Engineer-sealed shop drawings required for curtain wall systems.
