Broward Entry Door Requirements

Mandatory specifications for HVHZ compliance

📋

Wind Pressure Rating

FBC 2024 Section 1626

Entry doors must withstand design wind pressures based on:

Exposure Category C or D

Risk Category II minimum

Typical requirement: +75/-90 PSF for coastal Broward locations with 180 mph design wind speed.

🎯

Impact Testing Protocol

TAS 201, 202, 203

Large missile impact testing required:

9-lb 2x4 @ 50 fps

Door must remain in frame and operable after impact. Cyclic pressure testing follows with 9,000 cycles at design pressure.

🔒

Hardware Requirements

AAMA/WDMA/CSA 101

Multi-point locking system with:

Minimum 3 engagement points

Hinges rated for 400 lb minimum door weight. Reinforced strike plates with 3" screws into framing.

📜

Product Approval

Miami-Dade NOA Required

Doors must have current Notice of Acceptance:

Miami-Dade County NOA

Or Florida Product Approval with HVHZ designation. Installation per manufacturer specifications with engineer-sealed shop drawings.

Before vs After: Installation Details

What changes when upgrading to impact-rated entry doors

BEFORE

Standard Door Installation

  • ✗ Single-point deadbolt, standard strike plate with 1" screws
  • ✗ Standard 1-3/8" hollow core or basic solid door
  • ✗ Decorative hinges rated for 100-150 lbs
  • ✗ Wood frame with minimal reinforcement
  • ✗ Standard weatherstripping, basic threshold
  • ✗ No product approval or testing certification
AFTER

Impact-Rated Installation

  • ✓ Multi-point locking with reinforced strike and 3" structural screws
  • ✓ 1-3/4" solid core fiberglass or steel with impact glazing
  • ✓ Heavy-duty ball bearing hinges rated 400+ lbs
  • ✓ Reinforced frame anchored to structure per NOA
  • ✓ Compression weatherseal, ADA-compliant threshold
  • ✓ Miami-Dade NOA with engineer-sealed installation
