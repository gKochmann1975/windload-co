Common Questions

Pivot Door FAQs

Can pivot doors meet Miami-Dade HVHZ requirements?

Yes, but with significant engineering challenges. Pivot doors can meet HVHZ requirements but typically achieve lower design pressures than traditional hinged doors due to the single-point pivot mechanism. Limited NOA options exist, often requiring project-specific engineering approval.

What is the maximum size for impact pivot doors?

Impact pivot doors typically max out at 48-60 inches wide and 10 feet tall for HVHZ applications. Larger sizes require custom engineering and typically achieve lower design pressures. Door weight is limited by the pivot hardware capacity.

What pivot hardware is approved for HVHZ?

HVHZ-approved pivot hardware must be tested as a complete assembly with the door. Common approved systems include FritsJurgens System M and custom-engineered solutions with floor and header mounts. Always verify the complete assembly has NOA coverage.

What is the cost difference for impact pivot doors?

Impact pivot doors range from $6,000 to $25,000+ installed depending on size and configuration. The high cost reflects specialized pivot hardware ($2,000-5,000), heavy laminated glass, custom aluminum frames, and engineering required for HVHZ compliance.
