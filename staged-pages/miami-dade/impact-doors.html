Common Questions

Impact Door FAQs

What impact rating is required in Miami-Dade HVHZ?

Impact doors in Miami-Dade HVHZ must pass both large and small missile impact tests per ASTM E1886/E1996. They must have a valid Miami-Dade NOA and be rated for the required design pressure based on building height and exposure category.

What is the minimum design pressure for impact doors?

Minimum design pressure depends on location and building height. Typical requirements range from +/-45 PSF for low-rise residential to +/-75 PSF or higher for commercial applications and upper floors of high-rise buildings.

Are all impact doors NOA approved for Miami-Dade?

No. Not all impact-rated doors have Miami-Dade NOA approval. Florida Product Approval alone is not sufficient for HVHZ areas. Always verify the specific NOA number and ensure it covers your required design pressure and size combination.

What is the cost difference between impact door types?

Single swing impact doors range from $1,200-2,500 installed. Sliding impact doors cost $2,500-5,000 for standard sizes. Pivot impact doors are the most expensive at $4,000-12,000+ due to specialized hardware and engineering requirements.
