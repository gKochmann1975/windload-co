Common Questions

Storefront System FAQs

What storefront system is best for Miami-Dade HVHZ?

For most commercial applications in HVHZ, aluminum storefront systems offer the best balance of cost, performance, and availability. Steel systems are preferred for high-security needs or extreme spans, while structural glazing suits premium architectural projects where aesthetics are paramount.

Do all storefront systems require NOA approval?

Yes. All storefront systems installed in Miami-Dade HVHZ must have a valid Notice of Acceptance (NOA) from Miami-Dade County Product Control. Systems with only Florida Product Approval are not sufficient for HVHZ areas. Structural glazing may require project-specific engineering approval.

What wind speed must storefront systems withstand?

Storefront systems in Miami-Dade HVHZ must be designed for 180+ mph wind speeds. The actual design pressure depends on building height, exposure category, and internal pressure classification. Typical design pressures range from ±50 PSF to ±90 PSF for commercial applications.

What is the cost difference between system types?

Aluminum storefront systems range from $45-75/SF installed. Steel systems cost $85-120/SF due to material and fabrication costs. Structural glazing systems range from $120-200/SF for the frameless aesthetic and complex engineering required.
