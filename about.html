Our Story

WindLoad.co began in 2002 with a simple mission: make structural wind load calculations accessible, accurate, and affordable for everyone in the building industry.

What started as a specialized consulting service has evolved into the most comprehensive wind load calculation platform in the industry. We've processed over 50,000 calculations across all 50 states, earning the trust of professionals nationwide.

Our team combines deep engineering expertise with modern technology to deliver calculations that meet the strictest code requirements while being practical and easy to understand.

Why Professionals Choose Us

In an industry where accuracy is non-negotiable, we've built our reputation on delivering calculations you can trust. Every result is generated using the latest ASCE 7 standards and verified against real-world applications.

We understand that your time is valuable. That's why we've invested in technology that delivers results in minutes, not days—without sacrificing the engineering rigor that professionals demand.
