## Notes

- Window products in HVHZ require Large and Small Missile Impact rating
- MDP ratings shown are maximum tested values
- Actual installation may have lower ratings depending on size and configuration
- Always check full NOA document for specific size/configuration limits

## Design Pressure Guide

For typical Miami-Dade residential projects (180 mph, Exposure C/D):
- Required DP typically ranges from +40/-50 to +70/-90 psf
- Higher floors and larger openings require higher ratings
- Corner zones require higher ratings than field of wall
