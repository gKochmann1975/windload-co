## Roofing by Deck Type

### Over Concrete Deck (Highest Ratings)
- Modified Bitumen: up to 536.5 psf
- Single Ply PVC: up to 615 psf
- Waterproofing: up to 810 psf

### Over Steel Deck
- Modified Bitumen: up to 195 psf
- Single Ply PVC: up to 127.5 psf
- Metal Panels: up to 204.25 psf

### Over Wood Deck
- Metal Panels: up to 204.25 psf
- Modified Bitumen: up to 105 psf
- Single Ply: up to 112.5 psf

## Notes

- Roofing is the largest category in Miami-Dade NOA database
- MDP- (negative/uplift) is critical for roofing - wind creates suction
- Deck type significantly affects maximum ratings
- Many roofing products have MDP+ = 0 (not designed for positive pressure)
- Fastener patterns and insulation affect final ratings
