## Shutter Types Comparison

| Type | Pros | Cons | Typical MDP Range |
|------|------|------|-------------------|
| **Accordion** | Quick deployment, permanent mount | Manual operation, visible tracks | +100/-150 to +150/-200 |
| **Rollup** | Motorized option, clean look | Higher cost, mechanical components | +150/-180 to +200/-200 |
| **Colonial** | Decorative, always visible | Manual, limited sizes | +80/-100 to +120/-150 |
| **Bahama** | Shade benefit, decorative | Manual, limited protection | +60/-80 to +100/-120 |
| **Storm Panels** | Low cost, removable | Storage required, installation time | +80/-100 to +150/-180 |

## Notes

- Shutters are a popular alternative to impact windows
- All HVHZ shutters require Large and Small Missile Impact rating
- Rollup shutters typically have highest design pressure ratings
- Accordion shutters offer good balance of cost and performance
- MDP ratings decrease with larger shutter sizes

## Design Pressure Guide

For shutters protecting windows in Miami-Dade (180 mph):
- Small windows (< 20 sq ft): +50/-60 psf typical
- Medium windows (20-40 sq ft): +60/-80 psf typical
- Large openings (> 40 sq ft): +70/-100+ psf typical
- Corner zones: Add 20-30% to field requirements

## Installation Notes

- Proper anchorage is critical - NOA specifies fastener requirements
- Header and sill track attachment must meet NOA specifications
- Shutter spans have maximum width limits per NOA
- Always reference NOA for specific installation requirements
