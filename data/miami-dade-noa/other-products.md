## Notes

- These products are often overlooked but require NOA in HVHZ
- Wall louvers and vents can be entry points for wind and water
- HVAC roof curbs must resist uplift forces
- Glass railings on balconies require impact rating in HVHZ
- Material certifications (like PVC extrusions) support window/door NOAs

## Why These Products Matter

**Louvers & Vents:**
- Weak points in building envelope
- Impact-rated louvers prevent debris penetration
- Required at mechanical rooms, parking garages, stairwells

**Roof Curbs:**
- Support HVAC equipment on roofs
- Failure allows water intrusion and equipment damage
- Must match roofing system design pressures

**Glass Railings:**
- Exposed to full wind loads on balconies
- Impact rating prevents failure from windborne debris
- Failure creates hazard and building envelope breach
