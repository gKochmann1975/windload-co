## Notes

- Garage doors are critical for HVHZ - failure allows wind into building envelope
- Sectional doors require impact rating for HVHZ installation
- Width affects maximum design pressure rating
- Always verify NOA includes your specific door size

## Design Pressure Guide

For garage doors in Miami-Dade (180 mph):
- Single car garage (8-10ft wide): typically +35/-45 psf required
- Double car garage (16-18ft wide): typically +40/-55 psf required
- Actual requirements depend on building height and exposure
