## Data Quality Notes

1. **Expiration dates** - Many NOAs expire in 2026, need renewal monitoring
2. **MDP = 0** - Some products show 0 for untested directions (e.g., roofing positive pressure)
3. **Impact ratings** - Critical for fenestration; "Large and Small Missile Impact" required in HVHZ
4. **Partial data** - This sample doesn't include all products; full database has thousands more
