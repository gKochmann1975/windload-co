Impact vs Non-Impact Windows

Watch what happens when hurricane debris strikes. One shatters completely, the other holds together. This difference can mean life or death for your building's envelope.

Non-Impact Glass

Standard tempered/annealed glass
BREACH - ENVELOPE FAILED

Impact-Rated Glass

Laminated with PVB interlayer
PASS - ENVELOPE INTACT

Non-Impact Result

  • Glass shatters completely
  • Opening created for wind entry
  • Interior pressurizes rapidly
  • Roof lift-off risk increases
  • Water intrusion guaranteed

Impact-Rated Result

  • Glass cracks but stays intact
  • PVB interlayer holds shards
  • Building envelope maintained
  • Survives 9,000 pressure cycles
  • Water infiltration prevented

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about impact vs non-impact windows.

What's the difference between impact and non-impact windows?
+
Impact windows use laminated glass with a plastic interlayer (PVB) that holds the glass together when broken. Non-impact windows use standard tempered or annealed glass that shatters completely, creating an opening for wind and water to enter your building.
Do I need impact windows in Palm Beach County?
+
In HVHZ (High Velocity Hurricane Zone) areas of Palm Beach County, impact-rated products or approved shutters are required. In non-HVHZ areas, you can use non-impact windows with approved hurricane shutters for wind-borne debris protection.
How are impact windows tested?
+
Impact windows undergo large missile testing (9-lb 2x4 lumber at 50 fps) and small missile testing (10 steel balls at 80 fps) per ASTM E1886/E1996. After impact, they must survive 9,000 pressure cycles without creating an opening in the building envelope.
Are impact windows more expensive than non-impact?
+
Impact windows typically cost 50-100% more than comparable non-impact windows. However, they eliminate the need for separate hurricane shutters, provide year-round protection, and often reduce insurance premiums by 25-45%, offsetting much of the cost over time.
Can I use shutters instead of impact windows?
+
Yes, in most areas approved hurricane shutters provide equivalent protection to impact windows. The window/shutter combination must meet the required design pressure and debris impact ratings. However, shutters require deployment before storms and don't provide everyday security benefits.
