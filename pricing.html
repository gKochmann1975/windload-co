Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in a calculation?
Each calculation includes complete wind load analysis per ASCE 7-22, including velocity pressure, external and internal pressure coefficients, and final design pressures. You get a downloadable PDF report suitable for permit submission.
Is the PE seal required for permits?
It depends on your jurisdiction and project type. Many building departments accept our calculations without a PE stamp for simple structures. For larger projects or in jurisdictions that require engineering sign-off, our PE seal service provides the official stamp you need.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your Professional subscription at any time. You'll retain access until the end of your current billing period. All your saved calculations remain accessible.
Do you support HVHZ (Miami-Dade) calculations?
Yes! Our calculator fully supports High Velocity Hurricane Zone requirements for Miami-Dade and Broward counties. We automatically detect HVHZ locations and apply the correct calculation methods per FBC 2023.
